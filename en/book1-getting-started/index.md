# Book 1: Getting Started with Claude Code

> Written 2026-10-04.

> From installation to your first checked piece of work. No coding experience required.

This book is for anyone starting with Claude Code in late 2026: people who have never opened a terminal, and developers who have used chat assistants but not a coding agent. It follows the second edition of the handbook. The first edition (March 2026) taught you to approve every step an AI took. A lot has changed since then. Auto mode is now how most sessions start, the models can work for long stretches on their own, and the skill that matters most is checking the result, not reading every line. This book teaches the new basics in that order.

If you read the first edition, start with [What's New](/en/whats-new). The first edition is still online under `/v1/`.

## How to read this book

Read Parts I and II in order. They take about an hour and leave you with a working setup. After that, go to whichever chapter fits the job in front of you. Chapter 12, Check the Work, is the one to read even if you skip others: an agent that works on its own is only useful if you can tell when it got something wrong.

When you are comfortable here, [Book 2: Power User](/en/book2-advanced/) covers skills, subagents, hooks and context, and [Book 3: Architect](/en/book3-architect/) covers running many agents, containing them and measuring what they cost.
